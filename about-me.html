About Me


▶ Get to know me!

I'm a Game Designer based in Uppsala, focused on level and content design. I studied Game Design at PlaygroundSquad (2023-2025), where I primarily served as a Level Designer and QA Tester on two PlayStation 5 projects: Rocket Rider in Unreal Engine 5 and BEETLEBOT made on a in-house engine.
Since 2018 I've made content for The Road to 56, a Hearts of Iron IV mod with over 1,500,000 Steam subscribers. I script focus trees and seasonal events, and I fix the bugs players report on the mods Discord and subreddit. I started out writing missions for Team Fortress 2 community maps and running their playtests. I'm looking for work in level design, content design or QA, anywhere in Sweden or remote.
Feedback on my work is always welcome. You can reach me at eliasolsson.gamedev@gmail.com.
